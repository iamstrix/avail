The problem

Every empty day is ₱7,000 to ₱10,000 the resort doesn’t earn

Miranda’s Resort in Panlinlang, Arayat, Pampanga rents its whole private pool to one group a day: ₱7,000 on weekdays, ₱10,000 on weekends and holidays. It takes bookings only through its Facebook page. In peak season it fills 15 to 20 days a month. Off-season, only 5 to 7.

5–7 vs 15–20bookings a month, off-season vs peak
₱56k–₱150kless income in every off-season month
Business hoursthe owner answers every message alone, and only during business hours. Late replies have cost bookings.

Two reasons the days stay empty

1

Nobody knows the day is free

Empty days don’t get posted, so people only find out by asking. Most never ask.

2

Late replies lose the booking

A guest who messages at 10 PM gets an answer the next day, and by then has booked somewhere else.

Today’s tools don’t fit a resort that rents out whole days

  • Facebook’s Book Now buttonOffers 30-minute appointment slots, 47 of them a day.
  • Booking sitesTake about 15% of every booking, and are built for nightly stays, not day tours.
  • Facebook’s automatic repliesCan’t check which days are free, or work out the price for a group of 25.

Numbers are from the owner, from before the current renovation. The resort reopens after summer 2027. ₱56k–₱150k is 8 to 15 fewer bookings a month, times ₱7,000 to ₱10,000.

The solution

avail posts the empty days, and answers every guest right away

It works inside the resort’s own Facebook page and Messenger. Guests don’t need a new app, and the owner keeps selling the way they already do.

Fill empty days

The owner taps the empty days. avail writes the post.

avail writes a Facebook post for those days with the right price and a perk instead of a discount:

  • free breakfast for the group, or
  • late checkout, only when the next day is free.

It knows weekend and holiday rates, and mentions payday when it fits. The owner reads it, edits if needed, and posts with one tap.

Answer every guest

A guest asks at 10 PM. The exact price comes back in seconds.

avail reads the Taglish message: which day, overnight or day tour, how many people. It works out the exact price from the owner’s price list, and saves the day for that guest until the GCash downpayment comes in.

When the receipt arrives, the owner gets an alert, checks GCash and taps Confirm. The guest gets the confirmation on Messenger.
